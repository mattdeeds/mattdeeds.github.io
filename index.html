Selected work

pokeemerald-rp2350

A Game Boy Advance game ported from ARMv4T to Cortex-M33 and running natively on a $6 microcontroller. Not an emulator. GBA’s video hardware is reimplemented in software and ouput at 60 fps to HDMI.

Covered by AdafruitCRP2350HSTX

pico-remote-probe

A network-attached SWD debug probe on a single Pico 2 for remote flashing and debugging. The probe runs a GDB stub so a bare gdb-multiarch can flash and debug a target over the network. No host-side tooling required. Plus a host-free RTT viewer and an OpenOCD bridge for RISC-V cores.

Rustno_stdPIOsmoltcp

pico-10base-t-rs

10BASE-T Ethernet TX and RX bit-banged out of PIO and DMA. No MAC or PHY. Just a transceiver, magnetics, and software. Bridged to a TCP/IP stack to make the Pico 2 W into a working wireless router.

Covered by HackadayRustPIOsmoltcp

pico-llm

Transformer language model inference on bare metal with only 520 KB of RAM. The model weights are stream off a micro SD card through double-buffered DMA on one core. The other core runs quantised matrix multiplication. Tested with a custom 9M-parameter model and Qwen3 models. Write-up in progress.

CRP2350KiCad

Siri Remote dictation dongle

Uses the ESP32-S3 as a Bluetooth -> USB HID dongle to capture audio and button presses from the Siri Remote gen 3. The audio stream is decoded on the host machine and processed by a local ASR model. The output text is fed back to the dongle over USB and the dongle types the text back to the host allowing the remote to be used as a custom dictation tool.

ESP32-S3BLEOpus

Minnow E-reader

A tiny e-reader powered by a CR2450 coin-cell. Built to magnetically attach to the back of your phone and keep you focused on a single book. Instantly turns on and is ready to read unlike my experience with Linux based e-readers. Able to last years on a single coin-cell.

HBCam

A handheld monochrome camera inspired by the Game Boy Camera. PIO for frame capture, live viewfinder, dithering and palettes, gallery, and USB mass storage.
