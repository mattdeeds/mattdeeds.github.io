Selected work

pokeemerald-rp2350

A Game Boy Advance game ported from ARMv4T to Cortex-M33 and running natively on a $6 microcontroller. Not an emulator. GBA’s video hardware is reimplemented in software and ouput at 60 fps to HDMI.

Covered by AdafruitCRP2350HSTX

pico-remote-probe

A network-attached SWD debug probe on a single Pico 2 for remote flashing and debugging. The probe runs a GDB stub so a bare gdb-multiarch can flash and debug a target over the network with no host-side tooling. Plus a host-free RTT viewer and an OpenOCD bridge for RISC-V cores.

Rustno_stdPIOsmoltcp

pico-10base-t-rs

10BASE-T Ethernet bit-banged out of PIO and DMA. No MAC or PHY, just a transceiver and magnetics. Bridged to a TCP/IP stack to make the Pico 2 W into a working wireless router.

Covered by HackadayRustPIOsmoltcp

pico-llm

Transformer language model inference on bare metal with only 520 KB of RAM. Weights are stream off an SD card through double-buffered DMA on one core while the other runs quantised matmul. Runs a custom 9M-parameter model and a ported Qwen3-0.6B.

CRP2350custom PCB

Siri Remote (gen 3) protocol

Reverse engineering the third-generation Apple Siri Remote over BLE: GATT layout, button and clickpad encoding, and the Opus voice stream. The audio path has not been documented publicly before. Write-up in progress.

ESP32-S3NimBLEOpus

HBCam

A handheld monochrome camera in the spirit of the Game Boy Camera, from KiCad schematic through assembly to firmware: PIO frame capture, live viewfinder, dithering and palettes, gallery, and USB mass storage.
